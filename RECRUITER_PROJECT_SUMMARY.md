# Recruiter Project Summary

## Insurance Claims Analytics & Portfolio Monitoring

**Tools:** Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI-ready analytics, Statistics, Machine Learning

- Analyzed 12,002 insurance claims to identify portfolio, claim-value, channel, accident-site and operational patterns.
- Built fraud-rate segmentation and statistical diagnostics to surface relationships across claim characteristics.
- Developed a class-weighted Logistic Regression fraud-risk baseline and evaluated discrimination using ROC-AUC and precision–recall analysis.
- Automated management reporting through SQL KPI queries and an Excel MIS workflow.
- Created presentation-grade monitoring visuals covering claim concentration, monthly trends, fraud exposure, operational duration and model performance.
